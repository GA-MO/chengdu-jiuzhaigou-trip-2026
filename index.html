Tips and emergency numbers

  • Above 3,000 m, rest well, walk slowly and drink water. Bring medicine and canned oxygen. If you feel dizzy or short of breath, go down to lower ground right away.
  • Use Alipay / WeChat Pay and install a map app that works in China. Park and train tickets need your passport.
  • Dress in layers. Bring a thick jacket and gloves for Dagu Glacier. Carry power banks in your cabin bag.
